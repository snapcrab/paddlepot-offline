Start King of the Court

For offline use, save paddlepot-kotc-offline.html to your device and open it in a compatible browser that can run local files. Before relying on it at court, turn on airplane mode on the device you'll use, then try starting a round and saving a score.

  1. Choose Number of players to use Player 1, Player 2, and so on, or choose Enter player names and type one distinct name per line.
  2. Choose at least two courts and allow four players per court. Court 1 is the top court. Tap Preview round 1.
  3. Check the teams and the Sitting out list. Tap Shuffle to redo the opening draw. When you're ready, tap Start round 1 and read out the teams.

Score and start the next round

Enter both scores and tap Log score & finish on the court card. To save several entered scores together, tap Save all scores. Every court needs a winner. If a game is tied, play a deciding point before saving.

After every court is scored, check the next round's teams and sit-outs under Play. Read them out, then tap Start next round when everyone's ready.

To fix a result before starting the next round or ending the session, tap Modify score on the court card, then Save correction. The preview updates from the corrected score.

Move courts and rotate sit-outs

Winners move up the ladder toward Court 1 and losers move down. Top-court winners and bottom-court losers hold at their end of the ladder. KOTC fills courts in ladder order and tries to avoid repeating partners from the previous round.

When there are extra players, those who sat out the current round get first priority, followed by players returning from a break, then everyone else in wait order. Returning players keep their ladder position.

Manage players and release a round

In Players, use the availability switch when someone takes a break, returns or leaves. It applies to the next round. Tap + Add player for a late arrival. Leave the name blank to use the next unused player number. Late arrivals join at the bottom of the ladder.

If you started the round and need to change who's playing, tap Release allocation and confirm before saving any scores for that round. Update the roster or availability, then check the preview and start the same round again.

Check results and move a session

After all courts are scored, tap End session when you're done. In Results, open a round to see its games and sit-outs.

Longest Court 1 streak counts consecutive completed games on Court 1 for each player. Wins and losses both count. Sitting out pauses the streak. Playing a game on another court resets it.

Scores and session data stay in the browser. To move a session, choose Settings → Back up JSON, transfer the file, then choose Restore JSON in Settings or on the setup screen in the other copy of KOTC. Restore replaces the session there. Back up first if you want to keep it, or before tapping New session.
