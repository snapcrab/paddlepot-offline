# PaddlePot Offline v1.4.4 — Community release

PaddlePot Offline is a standalone doubles Mixer for clubs and volunteers. The original Mixer code is offered under the MIT licence, so you can use, adapt and share it, including commercially. The guide and PaddlePot branding are outside that grant. The embedded fonts keep their SIL Open Font License notices.

- Download the HTML to open it offline in a compatible browser, or get the ZIP with the same HTML, the licence and the updated PDF guide.
- The guide explains how to check the Mixer in airplane mode and move a session with **Back up JSON** and **Restore JSON**. Scores and session data stay in your browser.
- See [Contributing](https://github.com/snapcrab/paddlepot-offline/blob/v1.4.4/CONTRIBUTING.md) for ways to help.

This update keeps scoring, rotation, the local leaderboard, browser saves and JSON backups compatible with v1.4.3.

Since the last published download (v1.2.0), the Mixer has also gained bulk score saving, optional Winner and Loser racks, the ability to add courts during play, and colour cues on saved court scores.
